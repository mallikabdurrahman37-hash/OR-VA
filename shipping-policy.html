Shipping Policy

Delivery timeline

Every order is given an expected delivery date of 6 calendar days from the date the order is placed. This date is shown on your order confirmation and on your order tracking page.

Shipping charges

Shipping charges are set per product and shown clearly on each product page and again at checkout before you place your order. Where a product qualifies for free shipping, this is marked on the product listing. If your cart contains multiple items with different shipping charges, the highest applicable charge is applied once to the order.

Order tracking

Once your order has shipped, courier details and a tracking ID will appear on your order page. Before shipment, your order tracker will show its progress through our standard stages: Order Placed, Order Confirmed, Shipped, Out for Delivery, and Delivered.

Delays

We aim to deliver within the stated window every time. If a delay does occur, we'll post a note directly on your order page, along with a revised expected date where possible.

Payment on delivery

All orders are Cash on Delivery. You'll pay the total shown at checkout in cash when your order arrives.

Questions about a specific order's shipping? Reach us on our Contact page with your Order ID.
