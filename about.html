About ORÈVA

Made with restraint, on purpose.

ORÈVA is a small, independent storefront built around one idea: buy fewer, better things. We keep our range focused, our descriptions honest, and our process simple — from the product page to the box on your doorstep.

What we believe

Quality over quantity, always.

Every piece we list is chosen for how it's made, not just how it photographs. We'd rather sell you one thing you keep for years than five things you replace next season. That's the whole philosophy — nothing more complicated than that.

How we operate

ORÈVA is run directly, so when you reach out you're speaking with the person actually behind the store — not a call centre. We currently ship within India with Cash on Delivery, and every order comes with a clear, trackable delivery timeline.

Our promise

  • Product pages that tell you exactly what you're getting — real photos, real stock counts, real shipping costs.
  • Cash on Delivery, so you only pay once your order is in hand.
  • Direct, human support over WhatsApp, phone, or email.
  • Clear policies on shipping and cancellation, written in plain language.

Get in touch

Questions about a product, an order, or anything else — we're one message away. Visit our Contact page for every way to reach us.
