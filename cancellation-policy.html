Cancellation Policy

Before shipment

You may cancel an order at any time before it ships. Go to My Orders, open the order, and select Cancel Order. The cancellation is confirmed instantly and no payment is taken, since all orders are Cash on Delivery.

After shipment

Once an order has shipped, it can no longer be cancelled through the website or by request. This is enforced on our end regardless of what any button shows, so please reach out to us as early as possible if your circumstances change.

If you're unsure whether your order has shipped yet, check its status on the Track Order page — it updates in real time.

Need help?

If you have a specific situation not covered here, contact us directly and we'll do our best to help.
