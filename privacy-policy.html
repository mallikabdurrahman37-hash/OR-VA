Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

What we collect

When you create an account, browse, or place an order, we collect the information needed to run the store: your name, email, phone number, delivery address, and order details. If you sign in with Google, we receive the basic profile information Google shares with us. If you browse without an account, we use a temporary anonymous session so you can shop without being forced to sign in.

How we use it

  • To process, ship, and track your orders.
  • To communicate with you about your orders (confirmations, delays, delivery updates).
  • To respond to enquiries you send us directly.
  • To maintain your account, if you choose to create one.

How we store it

Your data is stored securely using Firebase, a Google Cloud service, with access restricted through backend security rules. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Payment information

All orders are Cash on Delivery, so we do not collect or store any online payment or card details.

Your choices

You can update your account details at any time, or contact us to request that your account and associated data be deleted.
